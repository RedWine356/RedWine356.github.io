About

I am a 4th-year Computer Science and Engineering undergrad at BUET. Right now I work on machine learning research: fairness in graph neural networks, ML security and efficient inference.

My current project, SFUGAG, adapts a graph neural network to a new graph fairly without source data or target labels. I worked out the approach and wrote the paper, which is under review. Before that I built the linear-programming core of GridWise and worked on network security, hardware and app projects for courses and competitions.

Away from the keyboard I play chess and watch a lot of movies. My favourite is The Prestige.

In progressMorningstaaar36/SFUGAG

SFUGAG

I worked out the source-free, label-free fair adaptation approach and wrote the paper

Fair graph learning: source-free, label-free fair adaptation of a graph neural network. Only a frozen source task encoder and a sensitive head cross from the source graph; on the target the method reads node features and edges only. Scored on accuracy, AUC and two fairness gaps (demographic parity, equalized odds).

ResultUnder review at AISTATS 2027
